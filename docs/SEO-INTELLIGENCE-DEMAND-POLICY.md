# SEO intelligence demand policy

`VALIDATED` requires a `PROVIDER_METRIC` keyword-metric signal. `INDICATED` means search-observed behavior exists without that metric. `NOT_VALIDATED` is reserved for inspected evidence that fails to establish demand; `UNKNOWN` means insufficient evidence. SERP presence, suggestions, PAA, competitor content, and model suggestions are never `VALIDATED` demand by themselves.

Opportunity confidence is descriptive: `WEAK` has little evidence; `EMERGING` has a problem or commercial signal; `PROMISING` additionally has validated demand and commercial evidence; `STRONG_EVIDENCE` is reserved for future independent search, demand, and commercial evidence. None means build.
